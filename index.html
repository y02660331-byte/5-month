💜 Happy 5 Month Anniversary, My Darling 🥰

We completed 5 months together. I know ki ye 5th month hum dono ke liye accha nahi gaya, especially tumhare liye. Par tum tension mat lo, time ke saath sab normal ho jayega. ❤️

Aur ek baat bataun? Main tumhare saamne apni kuch feelings express karna chahta hoon. Ye last week mere liye bahut hard raha hai. Tumhare bina mera sarda (survive) nahi hota… matlab tumhare bina mera mann hi nahi lagta.

Mere paas yahan mere dost hain, wo roz party bhi karte hain aur kabhi-kabhi main bhi kar leta hoon. Main roz khelne bhi jata hoon, aur ab bus mein peeche bhi baithta hoon. Aur tumhe pata hai, main jaan-boojhkar wahi wali seat pe baithta hoon jahan tum pehle mere milne se pehle baitha karti thi. 🥺

Sab kuch theek chal raha hai, bas mera mann nahi lag raha. Haan, apne doston se baat karke thoda khush ho jata hoon, par wo khushi bas temporary rehti hai.
